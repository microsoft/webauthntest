Privacy & Cookie Policy

Passkey Playground — a public testing tool for the WebAuthn / passkey APIs.

What this tool is

This site lets developers experiment with the WebAuthn / FIDO2 passkey APIs. It is provided for testing and educational purposes only. Please do not enter personal data or real names — choose a nickname as your username.

Cookies we use

We only use strictly necessary cookies required for the site to function:

Cookie Purpose Duration
uid Stores the username you choose so you stay signed in across visits. 1 year
webauthn_chal A signed, one-time challenge token set during passkey registration and authentication to secure the operation. Set by the server as an HttpOnly cookie. ~5 minutes

We do not use cookies or similar technologies for tracking, analytics, profiling, or advertising, and we do not share data with third parties for those purposes.

Data we store

When you register or authenticate a passkey, the credential details (such as the public key, credential ID, and authenticator metadata) are stored against your chosen username so the tool can display and reuse them. Your username is stored in hashed form on the server. No passwords or private keys ever leave your authenticator.

Your choices

  • These cookies are strictly necessary for the sign-in and passkey flows to work. By continuing to browse and sign in, you consent to their use.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing this site's cookies and site data in your browser, or by using your browser settings to block cookies for this site.
  • You can delete your stored credentials at any time using the Delete and Delete all credentials controls in the app.
