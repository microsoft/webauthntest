Privacy & Cookie Policy

Passkey Playground — a public testing tool for the WebAuthn / passkey APIs.

info This site is for testing and educational purposes only. Please do not enter personal data or real names — choose a random username.

science What this tool is

This site lets developers experiment with the WebAuthn / FIDO2 passkey APIs. It uses only strictly necessary cookies required for the sign-in and passkey flows to function. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for tracking, analytics, profiling, or advertising, and we do not share data with third parties for those purposes.

database Data we store

When you register or authenticate a passkey, the credential details (such as the public key, credential ID, and authenticator metadata) are stored against your chosen username so the tool can display and reuse them. Your username is stored in hashed form on the server. No passwords or private keys ever leave your authenticator.
