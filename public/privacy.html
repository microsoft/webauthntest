Privacy & Local Storage

Passkey Playground — a public testing tool for the WebAuthn / passkey APIs.

info This site is for testing and educational purposes only. Please do not enter personal data or real names — choose a random username.

science What this tool is

This site lets developers experiment with the WebAuthn / FIDO2 passkey APIs. The application runs entirely in your browser and does not use a server-side credential database. We do not use browser storage for tracking, analytics, profiling, or advertising.

database Data we store

Your chosen username is stored in localStorage. Credential details such as the public key, credential ID, authenticator metadata, and latest assertion details are stored in IndexedDB on this device. Challenges are generated locally and temporarily kept in sessionStorage. No passwords or private keys ever leave your authenticator.

tune Your choices

  • check_circle The stored data is scoped to this browser profile and website origin. It does not synchronize to another browser, device, or hostname.
  • check_circle You can remove all locally stored data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • check_circle You can delete your stored credentials at any time using the Delete and Delete all credentials controls in the app.
